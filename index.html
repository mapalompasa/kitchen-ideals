01 / Brand Philosophy

Built for the way
Zambians live.

Since 2015, Ideal Kitchens Wardrobe & Hardware Ltd has set the benchmark for custom cabinetry and comprehensive interior fit-outs across Lusaka.

Under the direct leadership of Director Choolwe Simuunza (Registration No. 130988), our studio combines international manufacturing disciplines — drawing material inspiration from Italy, Denmark, Germany, France, and Spain — with a profound understanding of Zambian homes, cooking styles, and architectural spaces.

Every board is E-1 Core certified to meet European Union environmental and non-toxic emission standards. We do not import flimsy flat-packs; we engineer robust, climate-resilient furniture built to endure Lusaka's climate without sagging, warping, or swelling.

05 / Methodology

From Blueprint
to Completion.

Our structured five-phase delivery model ensures total design fidelity, transparent milestone communication, and zero unexpected delays.

01

Discover

Initial consultation in Lusaka. We review your architectural drawings, spatial priorities, storage requirements, and finish preferences.

02

Measure

Precision on-site laser survey of your walls, ceiling heights, electrical conduit points, and plumbing outlets.

03

Design

Generation of detailed photorealistic 3D renders, exploded cabinetry drawings, and final material board approval.

04

Build

In-house CNC fabrication with E-1 certified boards, laser edge-banding, and pre-assembly quality control checks.

05

Install

White-glove site installation, plumbing and appliance integration, final hardware calibration, and 5-year warranty handover.
